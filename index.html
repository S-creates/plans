no-counting nutrition

Adjustment rule after 10-14 days

  • If waist and scale are both not moving, remove half a fist of carbs from one daily meal.
  • If workouts crash badly, add half a fist of carbs before or after training.
  • If hunger is severe, add vegetables first, then lean protein. Do not add random snacks.
  • If bodyweight drops too fast and strength falls, add one fist of carbs on training days.
  • Keep protein stable even on rest days.

sharper face rules

Reduce puffiness while fat comes off

The face often looks better before major fat loss because water retention drops. These rules make that happen without dehydration tricks.

1. Keep sodium consistent

Salt normal home meals. Avoid huge late-night swings from kebab, pizza, chips, instant noodles and heavy sauces during the 4-week push.

2. Potassium plus water

Daily: 2 fruits, 2 large veg servings and steady water. Potatoes, bananas, spinach, tomatoes, beans and yogurt are useful.

3. Protect sleep and evenings

No alcohol during the push if jawline is a priority. Finish the biggest meal 2-3 hours before bed and walk 10 minutes after dinner.

how to progress safely

The 4-week ramp

Progression is not random pain. It is more clean reps, harder leverage, longer pauses or shorter rest while joints feel good.

Week 1

Foundation. Learn the movement, keep 2-3 reps in reserve, use incline versions freely.

Week 2

Volume. Add one set to major movements. Same form, slightly more total work.

Week 3

Intensity. Slower negatives, 1.5 reps, pauses and harder angles. 0-2 reps in reserve.

Week 4

Density. More work in less time, plus max-clean tests to measure progress.
