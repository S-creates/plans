Part 1

Nutrition framework without counting

This is strict but practical. You eat normal foods, judge portions by hand size, and keep protein high enough that fat loss does not flatten your arms, shoulders and chest.

Use this at every main meal

Daily food rhythm

  • 3 main meals per day, each built around 2 palms of protein.
  • 1 high-protein snack only if hungry or if one meal was light.
  • Training days: keep carbs at breakfast/lunch or before/after training.
  • Rest days: cut carbs slightly, not protein.
  • No liquid calories during the 4-week push.
  • If weight and waist do not move after 10-14 days, remove half a fist of carbs from one meal.

Face definition

3 rules to reduce puffiness and reveal the jawline

You cannot choose where fat leaves first, but you can stop making the face look softer than it needs to. These rules attack water swings, inflammation and poor sleep.

1. Keep sodium consistent

Salt is not the enemy. Big swings are. Keep home meals consistent, and avoid late-night kebab, pizza, chips, instant noodles and salty processed meats during the 4-week push.

2. Push potassium and water

Daily: 2 fruits, 2 large vegetable servings, and steady water. Potatoes, bananas, beans, spinach, tomatoes and yogurt help fluid balance.

3. Protect the evening

Finish the biggest meal 2-3 hours before bed, avoid alcohol, walk 10 minutes after dinner, and sleep 7.5-9 hours. Your face will show it.

Part 2

4-week arms and chest home calisthenics program

Four days is ideal. If recovery is poor, use Days 1, 2 and 3 only. Every workout starts with the warm-up and ends with the finisher.

Warm-up before every session

  • Arm circles: 30 sec forward, 30 sec backward
  • Shoulder rolls: 20 reps
  • Scapular push-ups: 2 x 10
  • Easy incline push-ups: 1 x 10
  • Hip hinges: 15 reps
  • Dead bug: 1 x 8 per side
  • Marching, jumping jacks or light shadow boxing: 60 sec

Weekly split

  • Day 1: Chest, triceps and core
  • Day 2: Back, biceps and posture
  • Day 3: Shoulders, arms and chest
  • Day 4: Full upper pump and conditioning
  • Rest days: walk, posture reset, mobility, protein consistency
